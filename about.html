Who We Are

A people called to restore.

The Still Waters Church is a Christ-centred, Bible-believing family committed to raising people of faith, purpose and righteousness.

About Us

Welcome to Still Waters.

The Still Waters Church is a ministry committed to impacting the world with the gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ. We are a charismatic and Bible-believing church located at the heart of Wakefield city in the West Yorkshire region of the United Kingdom.

We have a mandate from God to raise men and women who will become pillars of righteousness and faith in this world. We believe that the lack of direction is the basic and fundamental issue that needs to be addressed at this stage of human history.

The world is at a very depraved stage spiritually and emotionally, especially in marriages and families, and it is only the Church of Jesus that has the answer to the confusion that presently runs in the world system.

Our Mandate

Leading people to restoration.

The Still Waters Church is totally committed to leading you to the fountain of restoration where everything that man has lost shall be totally restored in full.

We are a multi-cultural and multi-racial ministry. At The Still Waters Church you will experience inspired and relevant teachings, dynamic worship, uncommon love, result-oriented prayers, genuine relationship, care, blissful marriages, healings, and much more.

Our desire is not simply to gather people into a building, but to help people encounter God, discover direction, grow in faith and experience the restoring power of Jesus Christ in every area of life.
