A LETTER — FROM OUR LEAD PASTOR

Welcome to a place of still waters— where your soul can breathe.

Welcome to The Still Waters Church. We are truly grateful that you have found your way here, and we believe that no matter where life has brought you from, there is a place for you in God's presence.

Our desire is simple: to be a community where people encounter God, grow in His Word, build meaningful relationships, and discover the peace that comes from walking closely with Him.

Whether you are exploring faith, returning to church, or searching for a spiritual home, we invite you to come as you are. Our prayer is that here you will find hope for today, strength for tomorrow, and still waters for your soul.

Yours in faith,

Julius Olutokun
